Cashmere Hug Lipstick

Product — Still Life Photography
Client3CE / Stylenanda
Year2024
FinishCashmere Matte
ScopeProduct Photography, Still Life
Brief

The Cashmere Hug Lipstick was designed to be felt before it's worn. Shot at close range, the campaign isolates what is typically overlooked — the woven textile texture pressed into the bullet, the grain of matte pigment — and makes it the subject itself.

ProductCashmere Hug Lipstick — Multiple Shades
Note

The flatlay arranges the full shade range in a single frame — less a product shot than a palette of intention. The macro detail reveals the textile imprint on each bullet: the name 'cashmere' made visible before it's spoken.
