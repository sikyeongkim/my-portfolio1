Brief

Goodal's vitamin C line needed a visual register that felt botanical without tipping into the pastoral. The product is precise — a high-concentration serum with a single clear function. The imagery had to match that.

Note

Light was kept cool and even. Green against white, nothing layered. The tangerine reads as ingredient, not decoration.
